People sometimes ask whether someone who has hurt them is allowed to have a gun. In the U.S., there are laws that can restrict firearm possession in certain circumstances (for example, some protective orders or certain convictions), but the exact rules can be complex.

Plain-language guidance:

- The answer often depends on facts (what orders exist, what the legal status is, and what jurisdiction applies).
- If you are worried about immediate danger, your safety matters most—consider contacting emergency services or a hotline.

Practical next steps (non-legal-advice):

- If it is safe, consider talking with a local advocate or legal aid organization about safety planning and your local options.
