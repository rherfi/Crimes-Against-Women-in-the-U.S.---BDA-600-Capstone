People often ask whether VAWA-related protections and services can apply when abuse happened in a dating relationship (not a marriage). In many settings, “intimate partner” violence can include current or former dating partners.

Plain-language guidance:

- Many victim services and safety resources do not require marriage. An advocate can help you understand your options based on your situation and your location.
- Legal definitions vary by jurisdiction (state/federal/tribal). Some protections may depend on how the relationship is defined under the applicable law.
