Victim services can include confidential hotlines, advocacy, safety planning, shelter, counseling referrals, and help navigating systems (courts, housing, healthcare, school/work accommodations).

Plain-language guidance:

- You do not need to share graphic details to get support.
- An advocate can help you think through safer options and create a plan that fits your situation.
- Shelters and services may have changing availability; it’s normal to call more than one place.

If you want resource options:

- Share a city/state, county/state, or ZIP code.
- Optionally say what you’re looking for (shelter, legal aid, sexual assault support, tribal services, housing).
