“Jurisdiction” means which legal system has authority to investigate, prosecute, and handle a case. In the U.S., jurisdiction can involve state, federal, and tribal systems.

Plain-language context:

- Some situations in Indian Country can involve complex jurisdiction rules (for example, depending on where the harm occurred and whether people involved are tribal members or not).
- Some VAWA-related provisions address how certain cases can be handled in tribal courts, but the exact details are fact-specific.
