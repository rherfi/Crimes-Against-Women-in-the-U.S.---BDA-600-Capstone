The Violence Against Women Act (VAWA) is a U.S. federal law that supports prevention of violence, funds victim services, and strengthens protections for survivors. It has been reauthorized multiple times. A major reauthorization was signed in 2022.

In plain language, VAWA 2022 includes updates such as:

- Support for victim services (like counseling, advocacy, and shelter programs) through federal funding streams.
- Some expanded protections and program areas (for example, addressing underserved communities and improving access to services).
- Updates related to tribal jurisdiction and responses to violence in certain contexts.

Important limits:

- VAWA is not a single “one-size-fits-all” solution. What protections apply can depend on facts and the legal system involved (state, federal, or tribal).
- This bot provides general information and can help you find support options. It is not legal advice.
