This bot is a prototype support and information tool. It is **not** a therapist, lawyer, doctor, or emergency responder.

What it can do:

- Offer supportive, non-judgmental guidance in plain language.
- Help you think through practical next steps.
- List resources **only** from its connected dataset (and in this prototype, some entries are clearly marked “DEMO” and are not verified).

What it cannot do:

- Provide emergency response.
- Provide legal advice.
- Confirm whether a specific law applies to your exact situation.
- Verify a service’s real-time availability.

If you are in immediate danger or someone may be seriously harmed:

- Call 911 (U.S.) or your local emergency number.
- If you can do so safely, consider contacting a crisis hotline.
