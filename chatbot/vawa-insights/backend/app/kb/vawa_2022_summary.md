## Overview
The Violence Against Women Act (VAWA) was reauthorized in 2022. This prototype knowledge-base document is a **sample summary** to support early RAG behavior.

## Key ideas (high-level)
- VAWA supports programs addressing domestic violence, dating violence, sexual assault, and stalking.
- Changes in statutory language and program guidance can affect definitions and implementation across jurisdictions.

## Dating partners (conceptual)
In many analytic contexts, “dating partner” is a relationship category used in incident-based reporting. Policy changes may influence:
- which cases are prioritized for services and enforcement
- public awareness and reporting behaviors

This bot **does not assume** that a policy change *caused* a reporting change unless a model-based causal design is provided.
