# VAWA 2022 Reauthorization Overview

## Purpose

This document summarizes key updates introduced in the Violence Against Women Act (VAWA) Reauthorization Act of 2022.

---

## Overview

The 2022 reauthorization renewed and expanded federal programs addressing domestic violence, sexual assault, dating violence, and stalking. It continued support for existing programs while introducing new initiatives and policy updates.

---

## Core Areas Covered

VAWA programs broadly address:

* Domestic violence
* Sexual assault
* Dating violence
* Stalking
* Related offenses such as sex trafficking

These categories guide how services, funding, and prevention efforts are structured.

---

## Key Updates in 2022

### 1. Continued and Expanded Grant Programs

* Reauthorized major funding programs supporting victim services and law enforcement
* Continued support for programs like STOP grants and sexual assault services

---

### 2. New and Expanded Initiatives

* Programs to improve access to sexual assault forensic exams
* Efforts to address cyber-based abuse (e.g., nonconsensual image sharing)
* Increased emphasis on prevention and community-based responses

---

### 3. Support for Underserved Communities

* Expanded eligibility for certain groups, including Native Hawaiian communities
* Increased focus on culturally specific services

---

### 4. Tribal Jurisdiction Expansion

* Expanded authority for tribal courts to address certain crimes involving non-Native offenders
* Included additional offenses and pilot programs for Alaska Native communities

---

### 5. Trauma-Informed and Restorative Approaches

* Support for programs that prioritize victim-centered responses
* Introduction of restorative justice-style pilot programs

---

## Interpretation Notes

* VAWA 2022 focuses on **policy, funding, and services**, not incident-level data
* Changes in law do not directly indicate changes in reported crime levels
* The system’s metrics (e.g., incident counts) come from separate data sources

---

## Summary

The 2022 reauthorization modernized VAWA by expanding services, introducing new program areas, and increasing attention to underserved populations and prevention strategies.
