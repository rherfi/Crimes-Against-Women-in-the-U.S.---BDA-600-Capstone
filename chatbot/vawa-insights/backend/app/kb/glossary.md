# Core NIBRS Terms and Definitions

## Purpose

This glossary defines key terms used in the system. Definitions are based on NIBRS data structures but simplified for clarity.

---

## Incident

A single reported event recorded by law enforcement.
An incident can include multiple offenses, victims, and offenders.

---

## Offense

A specific type of crime that occurs within an incident.
An incident may include more than one offense.

---

## Offense Type

The classification of a crime (e.g., assault, theft).
Each offense is assigned a standardized code and category.

---

## Victim

A person or entity directly affected by an offense.
Victims may include individuals, businesses, or other organizations.

---

## Victim Type

A classification describing the kind of victim (e.g., individual, business, law enforcement).

---

## Offender

A person suspected of committing an offense.
Offender information may be unknown if no suspect is identified.

---

## Arrestee

A person taken into custody in connection with an offense.
Not all offenders are identified or arrested.

---

## Victim–Offender Relationship

Describes how the victim and offender are connected (e.g., intimate partner, acquaintance, stranger).
This information may be unknown.

---

## Incident Date

The date when the incident occurred or began.
If unknown, the report date may be used.

---

## Incident Hour

The time (hour) when the incident occurred or began.

---

## Attempted vs Completed

Indicates whether an offense was:

* **Attempted**: not successfully completed
* **Completed**: fully carried out

---

## Location Type

The type of place where the offense occurred (e.g., residence, street, business).

---

## Weapon Type

The type of weapon used in an offense, if applicable.

---

## Injury Type

The type of injury sustained by a victim, if any.

---

## Property Involved

Refers to property that was stolen, damaged, recovered, or otherwise involved in an incident.

---

## Property Value

The estimated dollar value of property involved in an incident.
If unknown, a placeholder value may be used in reporting.

---

## Drug/Narcotic Information

Includes data on suspected drug type, quantity, and measurement when relevant to an offense.

---

## Arrest Date

The date when an arrest occurred.

---

## Arrest Type

Describes how an offender was taken into custody (e.g., on-view arrest, warrant).

---

## Clearance

Indicates whether an incident has been resolved by law enforcement.

---

## Cleared by Exceptional Means

An incident may be cleared without an arrest if:

* the offender is identified
* but circumstances prevent arrest or prosecution

---

## Age (Reported)

Age may be recorded as:

* exact age
* estimated average
* range

In some cases, age may be unknown.

---

## Sex (Reported)

Recorded as:

* Male
* Female
* Unknown

These categories reflect reporting structure and may not capture gender identity.

---

## Race and Ethnicity (Reported)

Recorded using predefined categories.
These values may be missing or unknown depending on available information.

---

## Bias Motivation

Indicates whether an offense was motivated by bias (e.g., race, religion), if identified.

---

## Criminal Activity Type

Describes the nature of criminal behavior involved in an offense (e.g., possession, distribution).

---

## Circumstances

Contextual details about certain offenses (e.g., circumstances surrounding an assault or homicide).

---

## Notes on Interpretation

* Many fields may be **unknown or incomplete**
* Definitions reflect **data recording structure**, not full real-world complexity
* Some categories are simplified for reporting purposes
